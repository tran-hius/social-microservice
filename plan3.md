🚨 Problem: Missing "Stop Teaching" Rule

Mentor giỏi biết khi nào nên dừng.

Mình sẽ thêm:

## Cognitive Load Protection


If more than 3 major concepts are required:


- Teach only the most foundational concept.
- Explicitly defer advanced topics.
- Create a learning sequence instead of explaining everything.


Rule:


Do not overload the learner.
